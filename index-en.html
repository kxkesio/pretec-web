Who we are

Engineering, technical support and commercial backing

PRETEC Ingeniería SpA represents specialized international manufacturers in Chile. We support companies in the technical definition, commercial evaluation, implementation and operation of each solution, with specialized technical staff and a network of spare parts suppliers.

Application engineeringTechnical definition of the equipment according to process requirements, operating conditions and applicable regulations.
Commercial management and supplyTechnical-commercial proposals, coordination with the manufacturer and delivery to site.
Commissioning and supportInstallation supervision, commissioning, after-sales service, upgrades, retrofits and spare parts.
